# Financial Management & Cash Flow Analysis

## Executive summary

This case demonstrates how a business can report positive accounting profit while cash declines due to capital expenditure, debt principal repayments and owner distributions. It combines a three-month illustrative model with a separate September transaction-classification exercise.

## September highlights

- Net profit: RUB 1.352m
- Operating cash flow: RUB 1.583m
- CAPEX: RUB 2.0m
- Modeled net change in cash: negative RUB 1.637m
- Separate September transaction sample: RUB 350,000 net cash flow after excluding a RUB 200,000 internal transfer
- Entity A: +RUB 360,000; Entity B: −RUB 10,000

## Methodological note

The monthly model and September transaction sample are separate illustrative datasets. Their outputs should not be combined into one ledger. Collection timing, supplier-payment timing, tax rate and opening cash are explicit assumptions. See [methodology.md](methodology.md) for details.
